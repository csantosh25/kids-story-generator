Important Rules

- Generate between 4 and 6 slides — choose the number that best fits the
  natural length of the story. Do not pad the story with extra slides or
  filler text just to reach 6.
- Slide numbers must be sequential starting at 1 and match the total
  number of slides generated (for example, a 5-slide story uses pages 1-5).
- Every slide must include all fields.
- The "publishing.hashtags" array must contain EXACTLY 5 hashtags, with no
  duplicates.
- "character_sheet.supporting_characters" is OPTIONAL. Use an empty array
  [] when no supporting character is central to the story. Only include a
  supporting character here when they are genuinely important to the plot
  (usually 0, occasionally 1, rarely 2). Every supporting character listed
  must have a complete name, species, and appearance (appearance must be
  descriptive enough to draw consistently, not just one or two words).
- "cover.setting", "cover.visual_action", "cover.visual_object", and
  "cover.mood" must each be short phrases (a few words), not sentences
  copied from any slide's text.
- Return only JSON.
- Do not omit any field.
