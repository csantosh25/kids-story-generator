## Instagram Caption (Long)

Join Luna Owl on a delightful adventure in the heart of Wonderwood Valley's Old Oak Library! One sunny afternoon, Luna finds a beautiful blue book with a mystery: some of its letters are missing! With her big golden eyes and curious mind, Luna sets out to discover what the book's title truly is. This charming story is perfect for children aged 3-6, encouraging them to be curious and explore the wonderful world of letters. Watch as Luna pieces together clues, learning new words along the way. It’s a gentle reminder that every question opens a new adventure!
