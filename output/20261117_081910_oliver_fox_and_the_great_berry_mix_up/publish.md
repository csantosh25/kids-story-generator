## Instagram Caption (Long)

In the sunny Blossom Meadow, Oliver Fox and Squeaky Squirrel are having a berry-picking adventure! But when a small accident causes Oliver's berries to spill, both friends face a challenge.

This heartwarming story teaches children about empathy, the importance of saying 'I'm sorry,' and the wonderful feeling of forgiving others. It shows how strong friendships are built on understanding and kindness, even when things go wrong.

Join Oliver and Squeaky as they discover that even after a little mix-up, true friends always find a way to make things right. It's a perfect tale to share with your little one about the gentle art of forgiveness. ✨
