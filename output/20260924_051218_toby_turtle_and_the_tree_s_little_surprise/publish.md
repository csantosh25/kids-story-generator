## Instagram Caption (Long)

What little surprise did Toby find by the big tree? This bright morning, Toby Turtle is enjoying his favourite place, Crystal Lake. He loves looking at the strong, old trees. He thinks about how they give us fresh air and are home to many small friends. As he explores, he finds something shiny that shouldn't be there. It's a little wrapper! Toby feels a bit sad because it's not good for the tree or its tiny residents. But Toby, true to his calm and thoughtful nature, knows exactly what to do. With his patient help, he makes the spot clean again. This story gently teaches children about caring for nature and why it's important to keep our surroundings clean. It's a lovely reminder that even small actions can make a big difference! 📚 Save this story for your next reading time.
