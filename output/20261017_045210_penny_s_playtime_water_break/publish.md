## Theme

Healthy Habits

---

## Moral

## Hook

## Instagram Caption (Short)

Penny Penguin loves to play, but sometimes she forgets a very important healthy habit! Join Penny as she discovers how drinking water helps her stay strong and happy all day long. A wonderful story about staying hydrated for active little ones!

## Instagram Caption (Long)

Penny Penguin is having the best time building a giant snow castle at Snowy Shores! She's rolling snowballs and stacking them high, full of energy. But after a while, even sporty Penny starts to feel a little tired and her throat feels dry. Can she remember the healthy habit she needs to feel strong again? This sweet story teaches children the importance of drinking water to keep their bodies and minds healthy, even during fun playtime!
