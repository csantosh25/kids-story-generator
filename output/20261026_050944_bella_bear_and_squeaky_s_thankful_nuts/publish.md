Join Bella Bear in Wonderwood Valley's Sunny Forest! One beautiful morning, Bella saw her friend Squeaky Squirrel. He was feeling very sad because the best nuts were too high for him to reach. Bella, with her kind heart, knew exactly what to do to help her little friend.

This heartwarming story shows how a simple act of kindness can make a big difference. And when Squeaky says "thank you," everyone feels happy! It's a gentle reminder for our little ones about empathy and the joy of showing appreciation.
