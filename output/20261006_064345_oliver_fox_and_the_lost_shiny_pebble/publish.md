What happens when a friend can't find their most prized possession? 🤔 One beautiful morning in Blossom Meadow, Oliver Fox meets his friend Pip Sparrow, who is very upset. Pip has lost her most favourite shiny pebble! Oliver wants to help right away, but Pip is chirping so fast, it's hard to understand. Oliver learns that by patiently listening to every little clue, he can truly help his friend. This heartwarming adventure teaches children the importance of listening carefully to their friends and how it makes friendships even stronger.
